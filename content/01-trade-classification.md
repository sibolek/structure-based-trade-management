# Trade Classification

## Scalp
- Allowed at a defined reaction level.
- Stop is tight and tactical.
- Target is nearest opposing micro-structure.
- Manage for immediate response.

## Structure Trade
- Allowed after setup confirms at a structural area.
- Stop is beyond the structure that proves the thesis wrong.
- Target is the next meaningful structural level.
- Manage by structure, not open P&L.

## Swing
- Allowed only with higher-timeframe structure.
- Stop is beyond higher-timeframe invalidation.
- Target is major opposing structure.
- Manage at structural checkpoints.

## A+ Exception
- Rare.
- Requires full checklist.
- Correct structural stop still applies.
- No improvising after entry.

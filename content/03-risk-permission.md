# Risk Permission

The setup earns attention.

The stop grants permission.

The target justifies risk.

The plan controls holding.

## Tiers
- R0: no trade. Stop is unknown, unaffordable, or emotional.
- R1: tactical. Tight stop, fast target, scalp rules.
- R2: standard. Structural stop fits normal risk.
- R3: reduced. Wider structural stop is allowed only with reduced size.

Wider risk is allowed only when it is the correct structural stop and size is reduced.
